Co-op Platformer (prototype)

Opens the game on this screen with a QR code. Everyone scans it with their phone to get a controller.

Move: arrows / A D. Jump: space / W / up (hold = high, tap = hop). Stand on friends, push them, get the key to the door, then everyone enter the door. M = mute.

Turn your phone sideways to play
(or use the Controller page on your phone for the one-screen mode)
